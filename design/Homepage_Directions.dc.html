1One homepage, ten design directions

Same product, same synthetic scenario, same facts on every screen: 18 open casualties, 6 lift assets, 31U LTOWB forward, reachback degraded, one escalation on the board. What differs is the UX thesis — what the homepage decides to put first, and how a commander reads it. Pick a direction and I build the full solution on it.
